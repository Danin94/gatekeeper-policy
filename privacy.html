Privacy Policy for GATEKEEPER

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Who we are

GATEKEEPER is Tangen Hospitality’s in-house Android app for checking IDs at four venues: Fast Eddie’s, Globe, Borracho, and Bigfoot.

What the app does

Staff use GATEKEEPER on staff-held Zebra scanners. The app scans the barcode on a driver’s license to check whether the guest is 21 or older, whether the ID has expired, and whether that ID has already been scanned.

What stays on the scanner

Scan records stay on the scanner. Names, ID numbers, dates of birth, addresses, weight, and images do not leave the device.

What is uploaded

Each night, the app uploads aggregate counts only to the operator’s own Google Drive account (gatekeeper.repo@gmail.com). The app uses Google’s drive.file permission, which lets it access only the files it creates.

Those counts include:

  • totals
  • accepted
  • denied
  • underage
  • expired
  • repeats
  • hourly counts

They do not include personal information from an ID.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to gatekeeper.repo@gmail.com.
